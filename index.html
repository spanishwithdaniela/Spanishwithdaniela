What a Spanish morning looks like

A small group for expat women while the kids are at school. No textbooks to wade through, no pressure to be perfect. Just good coffee and a lot of Spanish.

  1. After drop-off

    Kids are at school. Come straight from the gate.

  2. Coffee first

    We catch up, in Spanish as much as you can and English when you need it.

  3. Real conversation

    Ordering at a restaurant, planning a trip, helping with your kids' Spanish homework. Things you'll actually say.

  4. Home before pick-up

    You leave with phrases to use this week, and a group you'll look forward to seeing again.
